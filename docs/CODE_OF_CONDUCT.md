# Code of conduct

Shepherd is built in the open, and we want everyone who takes part (in issues, pull requests,
reviews and anywhere else the project is discussed) to feel welcome and safe, whatever their
background, identity or experience.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, and assume good intent.
- Give and take criticism of the work, not the person.
- Accept that a maintainer may say no to a change, with reasons.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, slurs or sexual language and imagery.
- Personal or political attacks, trolling, or deliberately derailing a discussion.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Anything else a reasonable person would find unwelcome in a professional setting.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to the maintainer through the repository's
[Security tab](https://github.com/Flexipie/shepherd/security) (**Report a vulnerability**, and say
it is a conduct report). Reports are kept confidential. You can also report a comment to GitHub
itself with **Report content**.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, commits and issues that break this code, and may warn
or ban someone from the project, temporarily or for good.
